# Scoped development v1 — frozen purpose and source gates

7 November 2026. Consumed synthetic cq-p01/cq-p02 fixtures; not unseen benchmark
facts. Test actual source selection/composition after approved application-owned
scoped disclosures and A's explicit callback activation. Previous failures remain
failed; no corrected or replayed output is credited as fresh success.

## Limits and sequence

- cq-p01 once; cq-p02 once only after every first-case gate passes.
- At most four main-model calls and one NeMo wrapper invocation per case; total
  eight main calls plus two NeMo invocations. The callback currently needs one
  mapper and one composer request when evidence supports claims.
- Each owned operator case has 180 seconds including integrity/preparation and
  child CLI startup, retrieval, generation, safety and persistence. Between-case
  source adjudication is separate from execution time.
- No retries, warmups, repair loops, Ragas, hosted inference or Databricks work.
  Local inference remains serialized. No CQ1 run, approval/export, publication,
  deployment, reviewer contact or alert is authorized.
- Stop on any declared technical/source/completeness/title/safety/publication,
  identity or time/call-budget failure. Retain the failure and unattempted cases.
  A failure closes this allowance; unused calls cannot reopen it.

The paired questions, source records and branch bindings remain unchanged. The
sidecars explicitly bind requested scopes to the original question, corpus and
declared plan. Only the checked deadline reduction may produce an executed plan

The new mapper wire has no model-written gap text/offsets. Application gap wording
is checked as application behavior, not credited as raw-model generation. It must

not infer absence throughout the database from empty retrieval.

Require raw composer source support and completeness for these small development
cases. A fallback may remain in diagnostics/final draft, but cannot hide a raw
omission and pass the development completeness gate. No editorial correction is
allowed to rescue a generated result. Keep raw selection, raw composer,
application resolution/disclosures, source fallback and editorial text separate.

## Source adjudication and advancement

1. Confirm exact input/model/settings identity, normally finished responses,
   bounded execution and final safety. Final nonempty drafts must be persisted as
   a review-pending revision with source/unit/subtask and scope/deadline lineage.
2. Inspect every factual claim, scope and gap against the pinned source text.
   Schema success is not support. Record raw/final support/completeness, fallback,
   warnings, title, errors and unresolved questions; include failure findings.
3. Bind the assistant assessment to the exact result SHA using ledger `assess`.
   Only a pass permits cq-p02; a fail writes the immutable stopped marker.
4. Owner source/wording judgment remains separate. Do not invent identity, ratings,
   usefulness, checking time or acceptance. Later stored approval/export still
   requires that exact revision. Do not repeat scoring of historical technical
   failures or transfer owner acceptance automatically to new outputs.
